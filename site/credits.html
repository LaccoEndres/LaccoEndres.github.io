Deep Space Waifu reception

Checked November 4, 2026. The Nekomimi 98% positive figure is a historical milestone: SteamSpy lists a 98% userscore, and game developer Lottie Bevan’s article using 2019 review scores mentions the same figure. It is not presented as the game’s live current Steam score.

IsThereAnyDeal’s archived original-game listing reports 95% positive. Steambase’s Flat Justice archive reports 93% positive. Providers may use different dates and review pools; their adjusted scores should not be confused with the raw percentage of positive reviews. No claim is made that every follow-up has the same rating category.
